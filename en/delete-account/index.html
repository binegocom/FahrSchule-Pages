Delete Account & Data Erasure

Complete autonomy over your educational records · Contact: binego.com@gmail.com

Transparency Notice: FahrSchule Schweiz does not require user accounts, emails, or personal identification. All exam results, bookmarks, error lists, and settings are stored locally on your device in a sandboxed SQLite database. You can instantly and permanently purge all stored data using the methods below.

Step-by-Step Data Erasure Guide

1

In-App Reset (Instant Local Factory Wipe)

Purge all progress directly from the app interface:

  • Launch FahrSchule Schweiz on your phone.
  • Tap «Settings» in the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll down to the «DATA & STORAGE» section.
  • Tap the red button labeled «Reset Learning Progress & Cache».
  • Confirm the dialog. All local exam records, history, bookmarks, and streaks are immediately destroyed.
2

Operating System Removal (Uninstall Application)

To eliminate the application and all associated sandboxed databases:

  • On iOS (iPhone/iPad): Touch and hold the FahrSchule icon on your Home Screen → Select «Remove App» → Confirm «Delete App». iOS immediately shreds all sandboxed data.
  • On Android: Long press the app icon → Tap «App info» → «Storage & cache» → Tap «Clear storage» and then «Uninstall».
3
