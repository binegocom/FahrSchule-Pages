Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 January 2026 · Jurisdiction: Swiss FADP (revDSG) & EU GDPR · Contact: binego.com@gmail.com

Core Principle: FahrSchule Schweiz is an offline-first learning application engineered strictly under Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default. No user account, registration, personal email, phone number, or biometric identifier is required to use this software.

1. Data Controller

The entity responsible for data processing in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG, SR 235.1) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR):

FahrSchule Schweiz Platform
Operator: binego.com
Statutory Privacy Email: binego.com@gmail.com
Official Website: https://binegocom.github.io/FahrSchule-Pages/

2. Data Minimisation & Local Storage Architecture

All learning progress, test scores, answered theory questions, bookmarked questions, mistakes, day streaks, and configuration settings are stored strictly locally on your device (in local encrypted SQLite databases and protected sandbox storage).

  • Zero Account Creation: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create passwords.
  • Zero Remote Analytics: We do not operate tracking servers or background telemetry services capturing individual study behaviour.
  • Zero Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect or share Apple IDFA or Google GAID with third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

3. Data Processing Details

a) Local Device Operations

The application processes the following strictly on your device:

  • Selected licence category (e.g., Cat. B, A, C1, BPT 121);
  • Language preferences (German, French, Italian, English);
  • Exam simulation results, bookmarks, error history;
  • Timestamps used for local streak tracking and gamified XP progression.

b) In-App Purchases & Payments

If you purchase premium upgrades or additional question packs, the transaction is processed directly and exclusively by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play Store). We do not collect, view, or retain your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

c) Optional Educational Video Embeds (YouTube)

To enhance learning, optional educational video embeds or links to YouTube are available. These external requests only occur upon explicit user interaction (tapping to watch). YouTube data processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy with YouTube Privacy-Enhanced Mode (nocookie) enabled.

4. Statutory Legal Basis

This privacy policy conforms with:

  • Swiss FADP (revDSG, SR 235.1): Articles 6, 8, and 25–29;
  • EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679): Article 6(1)(b) (performance of contract) and Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests in software security);
  • Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1: Transparency in local data collection and complete data deletion protocols;
  • Google Play Store Data Safety: Full disclosure of local caching and payment routing.

5. Your Rights as a Learner / User

Under Swiss revDSG and EU GDPR, you are entitled to:

  • Right of Access & Information: Verify what data exists. Since all data resides locally on your device, it is directly visible within the app.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: Permanently wipe all data at any time (see Section 6).
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You may submit complaints to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB, Feldeggweg 1, CH-3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch).

6. Deletion of Data & Account Removal

You have full autonomy over your data:

  1. In-App Reset: Navigate to Settings → Reset Progress & Cache to immediately wipe all local records.
  2. Operating System App Removal: Uninstalling the application completely deletes all sandboxed databases and settings from your device.
  3. Email Verification: If you wish to receive written confirmation regarding data storage, email binego.com@gmail.com. For instructions, visit our Data Deletion Guide.
